# INTELLINA 2K26

The official website for **INTELLINA 2K26**, a national-level tech symposium run by the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at CIT, held on **March 6 & 7, 2026**.

This year we built it around a Stranger Things theme: the Upside Down, glitchy text, fog, film grain, and an intro that plays like the opening of a show ("A CIT Original Series").

**Live site:** https://intellina-2k26.netlify.app
(also at [intellina2k26.in](https://intellina2k26.in))

---

## My part

I'm one of the developers on the team. I worked on two sections:

### About
This covers the college (CIT), the AI & DS department, and Intellina itself. I wanted it to match the rest of the site and not feel like a page of plain text, so it uses the Stranger Things fonts and the campus image that flips into its Upside Down version. It also has some animated text, but I kept that light enough that the content is still easy to read.

### Event Gallery
This is a 3D dome of photos from last year's Intellina that you can drag around, instead of a plain grid. It holds 50+ photos.

A few things I had to deal with:
- The photos were large, so I had to watch the load time
- The dome took a few tries to work properly on phones
- The photos came from different phones and cameras, so the filenames are a mess. I know.
